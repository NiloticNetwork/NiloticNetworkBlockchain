## Traction Plan: Pilots and KPIs

### Pilots
- Schools/chamas: 3–5 partners, >1,000 wallets, recurring payments
- Corridor: KE–UG–SS micro‑remittance with capped spreads
- NGO cash‑transfer: offline redemption with audit trails

### KPIs
- Activation: wallets created, KYC tier distribution
- Usage: monthly active wallets, tx/wallet/month, median fee, failure rate
- Reliability: local ack latency, end‑to‑end finality latency, uptime
- Economics: contribution margin per corridor, agent revenue share paid

### Evidence
- Public dashboard (metrics), case studies, and references
