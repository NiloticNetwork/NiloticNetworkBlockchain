## Partnerships and Ecosystem Strategy

### Priority Partners
- Schools and education fintechs (fees collection)
- NGOs implementing cash‑transfer programs
- Mobile money agent networks and USSD aggregators
- FX/oracle providers for corridor rates

### Motions
- Co‑design pilots with 3–5 anchor partners
- Revenue‑share contracts for agents and corridor partners
- Provide reference integrations and SLAs for enterprises

### Developer Ecosystem
- Grants and bounties for useful dApps (invoicing, savings, reporting)
- Hackathons with local hubs and universities
