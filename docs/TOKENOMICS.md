## Tokenomics (Draft)

### Units
- Native token: Sulwe (SLW), precision: 1 SLW = 1,000,000 Lut
- Stable settlement units (SSU) for local currencies via custodial partners (off-chain reserves)

### Utility
- Fees: pay network fees in SLW or SSU (discounts for SLW)
- Staking: agents and validators stake SLW for routing priority and slashing guarantees
- Governance: protocol parameters and corridor approvals via stake‑weighted voting (later phase)

### Issuance and Distribution (initial testnet/mainnet v1)
- Genesis allocation: ops (10%), ecosystem/dev grants (20%), validator bootstrap (20%), community airdrop/pilots (10%), reserve (40%)
- Emissions: low, decaying issuance to validators/agents tied to throughput and reliability

### Economic Safety
- Fee floor to prevent spam; dynamic fee band under congestion
- Slashing for double‑sign or fraudulent agent behavior
- Transparent treasury with multi‑sig and on‑chain reporting

### FX Corridors
- Oracles provide FX rates; caps on spreads; circuit breakers for volatility

Note: This is a draft; subject to regulatory review and community input.
