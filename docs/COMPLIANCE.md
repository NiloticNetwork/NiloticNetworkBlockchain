## Regulatory Compliance Strategy

### Jurisdictions (initial focus)
- Kenya, Uganda, South Sudan; later: Rwanda, Tanzania

### Approach
- Sandbox applications where available; engage regulators early
- Tiered KYC: low‑risk tiers for small balances; enhanced due diligence for agents/enterprises
- AML: transaction monitoring heuristics, blacklists/sanctions screening via partners
- Data: privacy by design, retention and deletion policies, audit logs

### Controls
- Rate limits, fee floors, and velocity checks to reduce abuse
- Travel rule readiness for enterprise corridors via partners
- Incident response runbooks and disclosure policy

### Legal Structure
- Foundation for open‑source governance; operating entity for commercial services
