## Business Model and Revenue Streams

### Primary Revenue
- Transaction fees: low per‑tx fees with volume scale, capped for consumer use
- Corridor FX spread: transparent, capped spread on cross‑border corridors
- Agent network revenue share: commission on cash‑in/out and USSD usage

### Secondary Revenue
- API/SaaS for enterprises (SLAs, dedicated nodes, analytics)
- Compliance/KYC services for integrators (tiered pricing)
- White‑label wallets for schools/SMEs/NGOs

### Go‑To‑Market
- Pilot with schools/NGOs and regional SMEs; convert to paying contracts after KPIs
- Partner with mobile money agents and fintech aggregators for distribution
- Developer program and grants to seed ecosystem utilities

### Pricing Principles
- Predictable, sub‑cent consumer pricing; enterprise plans for throughput/SLAs
- Corridor spreads <= 2% all‑in (including partners)

### Unit Economics (initial targets)
- Median fee <$0.005, infra cost per tx <$0.002 at 20 TPS
- Positive contribution margin at 5 TPS sustained in two corridors
